The Book of the Well

村子底下有一口井。一千年來，守門人一個接一個下去封住它，沒有人回來過。

二十二歲那年，騎士團派她下去。她帶著一把發下來的劍，還有十年前對師父說過、再也收不回來的一句話。一路上，她撿起死人留下的信、帳本和刻在牆上的字。每往下走一層，就多知道一件沒有人肯告訴她的事。

很多年以後，她的紅髮已經灰白。她把一個孩子從井邊拉回來，孩子說，底下有人在叫自己的名字。於是她把那些舊紙拿出來，一天一層，從頭講起。

這一次，她什麼也不瞞。

Beneath the village lies the Well. For a thousand years, guardians have gone down to keep it sealed. Not one has returned.

At twenty-two, she is sent after them, with an issued sword and something she once said to her master and can never take back. On the way down she gathers what the dead left behind: letters, ledgers, words cut into walls. Every floor tells her one more thing no one would.

Many years later, her red hair gone grey, she pulls a child back from the edge of the Well. The child says someone below was calling. So she takes out the old papers and begins again, one floor a day.

This time, she hides nothing.
